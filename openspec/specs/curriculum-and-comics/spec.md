# Curriculum & Playable Comic Storyboard (`@trace spec:curriculum-and-comics`)

## Purpose

Defines the 12-Chapter Zero-to-One Arabic curriculum and playable comic narrative across three books: **Book I: Dubai with Nūr (`نُور`)**, **Book II: Beirut & Amman with Yāsmīn (`يَاسْمِين`)**, and **Book III: Cairo with Laylā (`لَيْلَى`)**.

## Requirements

### Requirement: 12-Chapter Playable Storyboard Across 3 Regions

The game SHALL include 12 complete playable chapters divided into 3 regional books, progressing monotonically from isolated letters and *ḥarakāt* to full sentences, culturally authentic café/rooftop invitations, dialect switching, and real-world MSA subtitle comprehension:

- **Book I — Dubai & Nūr (`نُور`): The Script & Gulf Hospitality (`Khalījī` + MSA)**
  - **Chapter 1 (`ch1_dxb_touchdown`):** *Touchdown at DXB* — Long vowels (`ا و ي`), Boat family (`ب ت ث`), *Harakāt* (`َ ِ ُ ْ`), first words (`باب` door, `توت` berries, `بيت` house), first encounter with **Nūr** at the airport terminal.
  - **Chapter 2 (`ch2_creek_abra`):** *Crossing Dubai Creek by Abra* — The 6 Non-Connectors (`ا د ذ ر ز و`), Spanish phonetic bridges (`ذ` = soft *d* in *almohada*, `ث` = *z/c* in *taza*, `ر` = *r/rr*), reading `دار` (home) and `زيت` (*zayt* $\rightarrow$ *aceite*), greeting Nūr on the water taxi.
  - **Chapter 3 (`ch3_alfahidi_cafe`):** *Qahwa & Calligraphy in Al-Fahidi* — Connectors (`ن م ل ك ه ة`) + Belly/Sibilant letters (`ج ح خ س ش`), *Shadda* (`ّ`) & *Tanwīn* (`ـاً`), Spanish `j` = `خ` and *azúcar* = `سكر`. Nūr shows Tlatoāni how letters drop their final tail to join; Tlatoāni orders `قهوة عربية` (*gahwa*) and blushes coral-red while Covi teases him.
  - **Chapter 4 (`ch4_sun_moon_museum`):** *Sun & Moon Letters at the Calligraphy Gallery* — Loops & Throat letters (`ف ق ع غ`), the `الـ` (*al-*) Definite Article secret revealed via Spanish *almohada* (Moon letter `م` keeps *L*) vs *azúcar/aceite* (Sun letters `س/ز` assimilate *L*!). Emirati polite phrases (*Shlōnik*, *Abghī gahwa*, *Mā ‘alēk amr*).

- **Book II — Beirut/Amman & Yāsmīn (`يَاسْمِين`): Full Abjad, Tillandsias & Courting (`Shāmī` + MSA)**
  - **Chapter 5 (`ch5_jasmine_bookshop`):** *The Jasmine & Tillandsia Bookshop* — Meeting **Yāsmīn** among hanging Tillandsias; the 4 Emphatic letters (`ص ض ط ظ` — **all 28 Arabic letters unlocked!**), Spanish cognates (*taza* = `طاسة`, *camisa* = `قميص`, *cero/cifra* = `صفر`, *alcalde* = `القاضي`, *hasta* = `حتى`).
  - **Chapter 6 (`ch6_bus_and_souk`):** *Bus Route & Souk Navigation* — Arabic numerals (`٠ ١ ٢ ٣ ٤ ٥ ٦ ٧ ٨ ٩`), bus/taxi survival (*Waddīnī*, *‘Ala janab hōn!*, *’Addēsh el-ḥisāb?*), decoding destination signs (`الشارع، السوق، المتحف، المطار`).
  - **Chapter 7 (`ch7_levantine_adab`):** *Poetry, Wit & Arab Courtship Etiquette* — Learning culturally authentic Levantine warmth and compliments (*Ahla w sahla*, *Tikram ‘aynak*, *Damik khafīf* [you're charming/witty], *Nawaritī el-makān* [you lit up the place], gender suffixes `-ak` vs `-ik`).
  - **Chapter 8 (`ch8_rooftop_invite`):** *Sunset Mint Tea & The Rooftop Invite* — Tlatoāni invites Yāsmīn out for an evening drink on the garden terrace (*"Ḥābbe nishrab shī sawa?"*, *"‘Alayya!"* — insisting on paying the bill per Arab *karam* hospitality) while Covi grins and mācron āi pops `🌸🍵✨` bubbles.

- **Book III — Cairo & Laylā (`لَيْلَى`): Roots, Dialect Switchboard & Media Subtitles (`Maṣrī` + MSA)**
  - **Chapter 9 (`ch9_nile_felucca`):** *Sunset Felucca with Laylā* — Meeting **Laylā** on the Nile; unlocking the **Triconsonantal Root System (*Jadhr* `جذر` & *Wazn* `وزن`)** with `ش-ر-ب` (*shariba / ashrab / mashrūb*), `ك-ت-ب` (*kitāb / maktab / kātib*), `ح-س-ب` (*ḥisāb*), and `ع-م-ل` (*‘amal*).
  - **Chapter 10 (`ch10_dialect_switchboard`):** *The Cairo–Beirut–Dubai Switchboard* — Tuning the ear to Egyptian `/g/` for `ج` (*gamīl*) and glottal `ق` (*’ahwa*), comparing how **Nūr** (*Abghī / Shlōnik*), **Yāsmīn** (*Baddī / Kīfik / Shū*), and **Laylā** (*‘Āyza / Izzayyik / Ēh*) express the exact same thoughts.
  - **Chapter 11 (`ch11_cairo_cinema_date`):** *Karkadēh & Indie Cinema in Zamalek* — Tlatoāni invites Laylā for hibiscus tea and a film screening (*"Tiḥibbī nishrab ḥāga sawa?"*, *"Mabsūṭ innī shuftik el-yōm"*); mastering the **10 MSA-to-Dialect Subtitle Bridges** (`أريد` $\leftrightarrow$ *baddī/‘āyiz/abghī*, `ماذا` $\leftrightarrow$ *shū/ēh*, `الآن` $\leftrightarrow$ *halla’/dilwa’tī/al-ḥīn*, `هنا` $\leftrightarrow$ *hōn/hēna*, `أذهب` $\leftrightarrow$ *b-arūḥ*).
  - **Chapter 12 (`ch12_media_watch_party`):** *Zero to One: The Rooftop Media Watch Party* — **Nūr**, **Yāsmīn**, and **Laylā** join **Tlatoāni**, **Covi**, and **mācron āi** on a starry terrace watching a live Arabic TV news bulletin and YouTube travel vlog with unvocalized/MSA subtitles—completing the Zero-to-One journey!

#### Scenario: Completing all 3 stages of a chapter

- **WHEN** the player reads the interactive comic panels, traces the chapter's featured Arabic glyph/word from right to left, and passes the contextual dialogue challenge
- **THEN** the next chapter unlocks immediately and the player's Notebook records every newly mastered letter, cognate, root, and regional phrase
