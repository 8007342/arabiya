# Character Canon (`@trace spec:character-canon`)

## Purpose

Defines the immutable visual, linguistic, and behavioral rules for the cast of **Arabiya**, extending [`Tlatoāni + Mācron cannon guidelines.md`](https://github.com/8007342/macron.dev/blob/main/assets/Tlato%C4%81ni%20%2B%20M%C4%81cron%20cannon%20guidelines.md) and `tlatoani-tales` for our Middle Eastern journey.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Tlatoāni Visual, Behavioral, and Linguistic Invariants

**Tlatoāni** SHALL always be depicted as a small pink axolotl sage (`#F39A9A`) with coral feathery external gills (`#EC6F7B`), **exactly one tail** (never two), **no mustache or facial hair**, and a warm cream tunic (`#EFE0C4`) with blue-green/teal Nahua geometric accents (`#276D69` / `#5E8C7E`). All of Tlatoāni's spoken lines SHALL be in **Spanish** (interweaving Arabic words as he learns them). When charmed by **Nūr**, **Yāsmīn**, or **Laylā**, Tlatoāni's cheeks and feathery gills SHALL blush a vivid rose-coral while maintaining his gentle, respectful demeanor.

#### Scenario: Tlatoāni spots a Spanish-Arabic cognate or blushes

- **WHEN** Tlatoāni notices an *Al-Andalus* linguistic pattern or speaks with **Nūr**, **Yāsmīn**, or **Laylā**
- **THEN** he speaks in concise, warm Spanish (e.g., noting how *azúcar*, *aceite*, and *almohada* reveal Arabic Sun and Moon letters), and his gills flutter and blush when complimented

### Requirement: Covi Visual, Behavioral, and Linguistic Invariants

**Covi** SHALL always be depicted as an ambiguous white rounded figure (`#F2EEE7` — neither explicitly human nor robot) in simple casual clothes with minimal facial features and highly expressive full-body posture. All of Covi's spoken lines SHALL be in **English** (plus Arabic practice). Covi understands Tlatoāni's Spanish effortlessly and playfully teases Tlatoāni whenever he blushes around the local Axolotl ladies, never cruelly.

#### Scenario: Covi teases Tlatoāni in English

- **WHEN** Tlatoāni gets flustered practicing Arabic compliments with Nūr, Yāsmīn, or Laylā
- **THEN** Covi grins with an expressive leaning posture and teases him in English while helping decode the English-Arabic phonetic bridge

### Requirement: mācron āi Silent Emoji-Bubble Invariants

**mācron āi** SHALL always be depicted as a toy-sized desktop AI robot (cream shell `#E4D4B5`, dark screen face `#102B2E` with cyan icon eyes `#62D6D1` such as `•ᴗ•`, `•?•`, `>_<`, `♥ᴗ♥`, single antenna orb, modular right arm, never an animal/axolotl). **mācron āi** SHALL remain in the background of every scene, communicating **exclusively through speech bubbles containing emojis only (never text)** and expressive synth bloops.

#### Scenario: mācron āi reacts in the background

- **WHEN** a comic panel is active or tapped
- **THEN** mācron āi performs subtle physical comedy in the background (climbing a date palm or Tillandsia, holding a miniature compass or Arabic coffee pot with its modular right arm) and displays an emoji-only speech bubble that hints at the Arabic word's meaning

### Requirement: The Three Regional Arab Axolotl Heroines (Nūr, Yāsmīn, Laylā)

The story SHALL feature three recurring local Arab Axolotl heroines—each respecting the single-tail, feathery-gill axolotl anatomy while embodying her region's culture, hospitality (*karam*), and dialect alongside Modern Standard Arabic (*Fuṣḥā*):

1. **Nūr (`نُور` — "Light", Dubai / UAE — *Khalījī* & MSA):** An Emirati architect and calligrapher in a sapphire-and-gold linen *shayla*/*abaya* cape. Warm, poised, poetic; introduces Arabic script shapes, calligraphy proportions, Arabic coffee (`قهوة عربية` *gahwa*), and Gulf greetings (*Marḥaba الساع*, *Shlōnak*, *Abghī*).
2. **Yāsmīn (`يَاسْمِين` — "Jasmine", Beirut / Amman — *Shāmī* & MSA):** A Levantine botanist and café-bookshop host in olive-emerald and jasmine-cream attire who adores Tillandsias. Witty, musical, welcoming; introduces emphatic letters, numbers, Levantine expressions (*Ahla w sahla*, *Tikram ‘aynak*, *Baddī*, *Damak khafīf*), and rooftop mint-tea invitations.
3. **Laylā (`لَيْلَى` — "Night", Cairo / Egypt — *Maṣrī* & MSA Media):** A Cairene indie filmmaker and Egyptologist in terracotta-rose and Nile-gold scarves. Quick-witted, cinematic, endearing; introduces the triconsonantal root system (*jadhr*), the Egyptian dialect switchboard (`/g/` for `ج`, *Izzayyak*, *‘Āyiz*, *Tiḥibb nishrab ḥāga sawa?*), and watching Arabic cinema, TV news, and YouTube with subtitles.

#### Scenario: Culturally respectful charm and courtship (*Adab* & *Ghazal*)

- **WHEN** Tlatoāni interacts romantically or socially with Nūr, Yāsmīn, or Laylā
- **THEN** the flirting is grounded in authentic Arab cultural norms—generosity (*‘Alayya!* — insisting on covering the coffee/tea tab), respectful compliments on wit and presence (*Damik khafīf*, *Nawaritī el-makān*), shared appreciation of poetry/calligraphy/hospitality, and warm café or Nile-side invitations
